Hash algorithms

MD5
128-bit · 32 hex chars
Fast integrity checks. Widely accepted in Indian courts.
SHA-1
160-bit · 40 hex chars
Common in older forensic tools and software.
SHA-256
256-bit · 64 hex chars
Recommended for modern legal filings. Strong collision resistance.
SHA-512
512-bit · 128 hex chars
Maximum strength for critical or high-value evidence files.
Legal basis: The Schedule [See section 63(4)(c)] of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 replaced Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. Part A is signed by the party producing the evidence; Part B is signed by the technical expert verifying it. Both require the hash value and algorithm to be stated. Attach the printed hash report to the signed certificate.
