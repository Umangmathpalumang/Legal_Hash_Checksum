Privacy

Files and practice records

File hashing runs in your browser. The case docket and time and billing entries are stored in your browser. Clearing site data can remove these records; use the export options to make backups.

Site requests and feedback

The server logs request details such as IP address, path, and response status. Some pages use Google Analytics. If you submit feedback or email support, we receive what you send and any contact information you include.

Legal Q&A

Questions and answers you submit are sent to the server for moderation and may be published. Avoid including confidential or identifying case information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support.
