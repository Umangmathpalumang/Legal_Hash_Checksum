Bail Bond (Form No. 45) Guide & FAQ

What is Form No. 45 under CrPC / BNSS?

Form No. 45 is the standard legal format used in Indian courts for a Bail Bond and Surety Bond. It is executed under Sections 436, 437, 438, and 441 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) / corresponding sections of the BNSS, ensuring the accused's attendance in court.

How to fill a bail bond format for court?

Filling out a bail bond format requires precise details to satisfy the court's registry. You must include:

  • Court & FIR Details: The presiding court, Police Station, FIR Number, and Under Section (e.g., 302 IPC or BNS equivalent).
  • Accused Details: Full name, parentage, address, and the bail amount set by the judge.
  • Surety Details: The guarantor's information, including property, vehicle, or FDR details proving their financial capacity to stand as surety.
