How to use your stamp

1Fill in your advocate details on the left panel and pick a shape and colour.
2Click Download PNG to save the stamp as a transparent PNG image.
3In the Bail Bond or Memo of Appearance tool, click ⬆ I Have a Stamp and upload the PNG.
4Drag the stamp on the document to position it, then Print / Save PDF.
5Click Save to Browser so your stamp is remembered the next time you visit.
