Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026  ·  Effective: 1 August 2024

Summary: We do not sell your data. Most tools work entirely in your browser — no data is sent to our servers unless you explicitly use a connected feature (Legal Q&A, Feedback). Payment data is handled exclusively by our payment partner (Razorpay / Cashfree) and never stored on our servers.

1. Who We Are

legalhashchecksum.com is a legal productivity platform for Indian advocates, operated by Legal Hash Checksum (India). We provide document generation, research, and practice management tools. For privacy inquiries, contact privacy@legalhashchecksum.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you do NOT give us (processed only in your browser):

  • Case management data (cases, hearings, time entries, notes)
  • Document drafts (vakalatnama, bail bonds, legal notices, stamp designs)
  • Hash verification data and evidence records
  • Court fee calculations and limitation period calculations

All of the above is stored exclusively in your device's browser storage (localStorage / IndexedDB) and never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Information we do collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, bar enrolment number (Pro subscribers only)
  • Payment information: Transaction ID, plan purchased, invoice amount. Raw card/UPI/bank details are never stored — they are processed entirely by Razorpay or Cashfree Payments under PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Usage analytics: Page views, feature usage counts, browser type, device type (via Google Analytics 4 — anonymised IP). No personally identifiable information is sent to GA4.
  • Feedback and Q&A: Text you voluntarily submit through the Feedback form or Legal Q&A feature.
  • Server logs: IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, path (retained for 30 days for security purposes).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and maintain the services you requested
  • To process payments and issue receipts / GST invoices
  • To send licence activation emails and renewal reminders
  • To respond to support requests and feedback
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To improve product features based on anonymised usage patterns

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. or Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd., both of which are RBI-authorised payment aggregators and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. When you make a payment:

  • You are redirected to a secure payment page hosted by the gateway.
  • We receive only a transaction confirmation, masked card/UPI reference, and the amount.
  • We never see, store, or transmit your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or net banking credentials.

5. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Payment partners: Razorpay / Cashfree — for processing transactions
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 — anonymised event data only
  • Legal obligation: If required by a court order or statutory authority under applicable Indian law
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with prior notice to subscribers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Cookies

We use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Session management (no personal data)
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _gid) — you may opt out via your browser settings or Google's opt-out tool.

We do not use advertising, retargeting, or social-media tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention

  • Browser-stored data: Retained on your device until you clear browser storage. We have no access to delete it.
  • Account & payment records: Retained for 7 years as required under the Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST law.
  • Server logs: 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Legal Q&A posts: Retained until you request deletion.

8. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erase your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Raise a grievance with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any right, email privacy@legalhashchecksum.com with subject "Data Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We implement: HTTPS/TLS on all connections, bcrypt hashing for any stored credentials, rate limiting and brute-force protection, regular dependency audits. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for legal professionals (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted data, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified by email to Pro subscribers and via a notice on the website. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

For all privacy-related queries: privacy@legalhashchecksum.com or visit our Contact page.
