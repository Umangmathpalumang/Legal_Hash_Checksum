Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026  ·  Applies to all paid subscriptions
Quick summary: Full refund within 7 days if you're not satisfied. Cancel anytime — no questions asked. Refunds processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your LHC Pro or LHC Firm subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing umang@legalhashchecksum.com.

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
  • You will continue to have Pro access until the period expires.
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation.
  • Your browser-local data (cases, documents) is unaffected by cancellation.

2. Refund Eligibility

Eligible for refund
First-time subscription, refund requested within 7 days of purchase, and the service was not used extensively (fewer than 10 document generations on the Pro tier).
Eligible for refund
Duplicate payment — charged twice for the same period. We will refund the duplicate charge in full.
Eligible for refund
Technical failure — payment deducted but subscription not activated, and we are unable to resolve within 48 hours.
Not eligible for refund
Refund requested after 7 days of the billing date (mid-period cancellations).
Not eligible for refund
Account suspended due to violation of our Terms & Conditions.
Considered case-by-case
Extended downtime (platform unavailable for more than 24 continuous hours in a billing period). Pro-rated credit or refund at our discretion.

3. How to Request a Refund

Email refunds@legalhashchecksum.com with:

  • Subject line: "Refund Request — [your registered email]"
  • Payment reference / transaction ID (from your receipt)
  • Date of purchase and reason for the refund request

We will acknowledge your request within 1 business day and process eligible refunds within 5–7 business days. Refunds are credited to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking).

4. Annual Subscriptions

Annual plan refunds follow the same 7-day window. After 7 days, no refund is issued for annual plans, but you will retain access until the annual period expires. We do not provide pro-rated refunds for unused months of an annual subscription.

5. Payment Gateway Refunds

Refunds are processed through the same payment gateway (Razorpay or Cashfree) used for your original payment. Bank processing times are outside our control and typically take 3–7 business days after we initiate the refund. For UPI payments, refunds usually arrive within 24–48 hours of initiation.

6. GST on Refunds

If a GST invoice was issued, the refund will include the full amount including GST. We will issue a credit note as required under the GST Act.

7. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us before initiating a chargeback with your bank, as chargebacks create additional processing overhead. We will work with you to resolve any billing dispute fairly and promptly.

8. Contact

Refund queries: refunds@legalhashchecksum.com
General support: umang@legalhashchecksum.com
Or visit our Contact page.
